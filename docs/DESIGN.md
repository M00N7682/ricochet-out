# Ricochet Out — design

## Pitch
Tap-away arrow puzzle (the genre of *Arrows – Puzzle Escape*, *Arrow Puzzle*),
with one rule of its own: **mirrors**. An arrow that meets a `/` or `\` mirror
turns 90° and keeps flying, so the path out is a line of sight you read, not a
straight row. Walls arrive at level 25.

## Rules
- Tap an arrow: it slides the way its head points, bending on mirrors.
- It leaves if the path reaches the edge; otherwise it bumps the first arrow or
  wall in the way, comes back, and costs a heart. Three hearts per level.
- Removing an arrow only ever frees cells, so a level can never deadlock.
- Every level is generated by *reverse insertion* (each new arrow must already
  be able to leave the board as built so far), so it is solvable by construction.
  `scripts/levelcheck.ts` checks 500 levels.

## Curve
- Board 4×5 at level 1 → 10×15 by ~level 80. Arrows 3 → ~40.
- Mirrors from level 3 (tip on first sight), walls from level 25.
- *Hard* every 10th level (more arrows, more blocking), *Super hard* every 25th.
- "Bite": the generator prefers arrows that block others, rising with level.

## Economy and retention
- Stars by mistakes (0 → 3★, 1 → 2★, else 1★). Stardust per clear
  (×2 hard, ×3 super), ×2 more for an optional rewarded ad.
- Boosters from level 4: hint (shows an arrow that can leave and its path),
  hammer (removes any arrow). Bought with stardust or one rewarded ad.
- Constellations: 30 levels light one constellation (오리온 → 용).
- Daily puzzle after level 5, with streak bonus.
- Six skins bought with stardust.

## Ads (when real ad units exist)
No interstitial before level 6 or in the first 180 s, then at most one per two
levels and 90 s, never right after a rewarded ad. The App Store build without
ad units shows **no ads**; rewarded buttons pay out immediately.

## Onboarding
First launch goes straight into level 1 with a pulsing ring on a free arrow and
one line of text. No boosters on screen until level 4.
