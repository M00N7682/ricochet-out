How to play

Tap an arrow and it flies the way it points. Mirrors bend its path by 90 degrees; if another arrow or a wall is in the way, you lose a heart. Clear every arrow off the board to win.

FAQ

I'm stuck. Every level is built to be solvable. The hint shows an arrow that can leave right now and its path; the hammer removes one arrow.

My progress is gone. Progress is stored on the device only and is reset if the app is deleted.
